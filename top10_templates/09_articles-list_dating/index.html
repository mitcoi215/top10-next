Ethical Non-Monogamy and Polyamory in Gay Dating Apps

Ethical non-monogamy is a relationship style where individuals have multiple consensual romantic or sexual partners. This contrasts with traditional monogamous relationships, where exclusivity is expected.

Polyamory, a subset of ethical non-monogamy, involves having intimate, loving relationships with multiple people. What sets polyamory apart is the emphasis on emotional connection and not just physical or sexual relationships.

In the realm of gay dating apps, these concepts have gained substantial traction. Apps like Feeld, OkCupid, and Tinder have adapted to this shift by providing features that allow users to explicitly state their non-monogamous or polyamorous preferences. This transparency facilitates easier connections among those who share similar relationship views and values.

If you practice ethical non-monogamy or are polyamorous, make sure to clearly write that at the start of your profile. In this way, you’ll make your needs clear to everyone checking you out.
